Tab protection

Add sites Chrome Window Parker should avoid disturbing. Enter one domain per line, such as meet.google.com. This covers every page on that site and its subdomains.

If an excluded site is the selected tab, that window will not park. Other windows can still park normally.

An excluded background tab does not prevent parking. In Discard immediately mode, Chrome Window Parker skips that tab. Chrome’s own Memory Saver may still unload it.

Advanced URL patterns

To exclude only part of a site, enter a full URL with *, meaning “any text”. For example, https://example.com/work/* covers pages under /work/ without excluding the rest of the site.

Calls, video and unsaved work

Exclude these sites or keep their tabs pinned. Chrome doesn’t reliably report screen sharing, Picture-in-Picture or unsaved edits to this extension. Parking pauses during active downloads.
