Tab protection

Sort A–Z · remove duplicates

One website per line, such as meet.google.com. Paste a site’s home address to exclude the whole site. To exclude only part of a site, use a full address starting with https://, with * where anything can match, such as https://example.com/work/*.

If an excluded site is the selected tab, that window will not park. Other windows can still park normally.

An excluded background tab does not prevent parking. In Discard immediately mode, Chrome Window Parker skips that tab. Chrome’s own Memory Saver may still unload it.

More about excluded sites

A website also covers addresses that end in it: example.com includes docs.example.com. In a full address, capital letters count, and https://example.com/work/* leaves the rest of example.com alone. For a local address with a number, such as localhost:3000, include the number.

Calls, video and unsaved work

Exclude these sites or keep their tabs pinned. Chrome doesn’t reliably report screen sharing, Picture-in-Picture or unsaved edits to this extension. Parking pauses during active downloads.
