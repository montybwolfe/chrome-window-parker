No login needed.
1. Toolbar icon > gear (Settings): set "Park windows after" to Custom, 1 minute. Save settings.
2. Open a second window with some pages; return to the first.
3. Within 2 minutes it shows "Window parked". Focus it for 2 seconds (or click Restore tab): the tab returns.
Pinned, audible or excluded tabs keep a window awake.
Settings > Sync is off by default. Tick a setting, a group or all. Settings changed here ask before taking synced values. Nothing goes to the developer.
